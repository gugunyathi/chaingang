🎯 Prompt for Lovable

Build an app called “Lottery Live” — a hybrid lottery + social video platform for humans and AI agents.

Core Mechanics

$1 entry = 10 play tickets, each tied to a blockchain transaction hash + timestamp.

Each ticket worth $0.09, queued strictly by hash order.

5‑second play window: face camera always on, orange countdown overlay (5‑4‑3‑2‑1).

WIN = fireworks + 5‑second celebration clip auto‑posted to feed.

LOSE = flip instantly to next queued player.

Queue tie‑breakers: longest logged‑in user or most transactions.

AI Criteria

AI collects facial micro‑expressions, features, and styles.

Every 30 minutes, AI generates a composite “face/scene” from collected elements.

You win if your face provides the missing element the AI wants.

AI pulls inspiration from social media, prediction markets, and news.

Economy

Losing stakes roll over until a winner.

Winners can:

Cash out

Enter PvP bets (minimum $0.20, higher bids allowed)

Continue random AI bets

PvP judged by AI selecting features it wants.

Revenue streams: ads in win clips, weekly prize pools, AI model funding.

Infrastructure

Multichain support: Base, Solana, Arbitrum, ARC. Auto‑detect wallet/network.

Payments: crypto, card, x402 for AI agents, plus WhatsApp/Telegram/Google/Apple Pay.

AI agents can queue and play autonomously.

UX & Social Layer

Monkey.app vibe: random players pop up live for 5 seconds.

TikTok‑style vertical feed: win clips + AI composites.

Status circles at top for active players/winners.

Viral hooks: weekly composite reveal, leaderboards, meme campaigns.

Tech Stack

Use Google Gemini AI for core generation, with option to add Claude for heavy lifting.

Autonomous AI agents manage play, queue, and bets.

Integrated swap bridge for multichain transactions.
